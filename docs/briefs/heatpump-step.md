# Brief: heat pump step (step 4 of the 4 + 1 chain)

Decided by Xander, Sat 7 Nov 2026 about 22:35 Munich. Branch `heatpump`. Ships when green. Draw only, no score change.

1. Rule unchanged: recommend a heat pump only when asked or the boiler is over 20 years (planners: 13 of 1,619). But every design shows the option as a variant: "With heat pump: 7.4 kW, +EUR X, payback Y years" (Reonic's own pitch: combine heat pumps with solar PV in proposals). Selecting it re-runs the chain through /api/refine with heatpump "include" (demand up by heat demand / SCOP, PV and battery re-sized). Step sentence when not recommended: "None, not asked. If asked: 7.4 kW, +EUR X." Economics keep the subsidy as the one labelled assumption; no legal framing.
2. Visual: ENVELOPE X-RAY on the 3D house, the single-zone version of Reonic's room cutaway (DIN treats a single-family house as one zone). Tint outer walls, roof, floor and windows by their share of heat loss in Reonic's grammar (orange large loss, blue small); windows as a typology share of wall area per build-year class; everything labelled "estimated from address". Camera: the heatpump pose on the west wall, pulled back so the whole envelope reads. Beside it the HEAT-LOSS BAR by element (outer wall, window, floor, ceiling, roof, ventilation, total W), the same numbers as the tint. Bivalence -6 C and hot water 0.5 kW per person shown as named constants on tap. Multi-unit buildings: no x-ray, "Apartment building: heat load per flat not estimated". Shading tints fade out when this step opens; the x-ray fades out when the planner moves to Offer.
3. Radiators and flow temperature: left out entirely (Xander). Not in the step, not in the site-visit note.
4. Engine: the per-element split (U x A x dT, ventilation by volume) and the window share already live on the envelope block of /api/explain/layers and /api/roof/model (faces, per-element W, ventilation_w, thermal_bridges_w, design_heat_load_kw, design_outdoor_c); ENGINE - Marquee adds whatever is missing (named constants bivalence_c -6, hot_water_kw_per_person 0.5, window share per face) and ships when green. The x-ray and the bar read the same fields.

Owned web files: `web/components/spatial/xray.ts`, `web/components/spatial/Envelope*.tsx`, `web/lib/envelope.ts`; minimal hooks in scene.ts, SpatialDemo.tsx and Chain.tsx (step 4 content and the heat pump variant row) taken last after a rebase on origin/web.
