# Business evidence for the deck

Sources checked 7 Nov 2026. "(vendor claim)" means a company talking about its own product: use it, but say whose claim it is. Gaps are listed at the end; nothing here is invented.

## Five numbers for the deck
1. Reonic: "from enquiry to offer in five minutes", 3,000+ installer customers (vendor claim). [reonic.com](https://reonic.com/)
2. A Reonic customer: "10 hours saved per person, every week"; another: a proposal in "10 minutes, at most a quarter of an hour" (vendor claim). [Reonic solar page](https://reonic.com/en-gb/industry/solar)
3. Germany is short of about 60,000 heating installers (ZVSHK, April 2023). [Deutsche Handwerks Zeitung](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=292248)
4. 299,000 heat pumps sold in Germany in 2025, up 55% (BWP). [Solarserver, Jan 2026](https://www.solarserver.de/2026/01/27/bwp-waermepumpen-absatz-um-55-prozent-in-2025-gestiegen/)
5. About 450,000 rooftop PV systems installed in 2025; electrical trade firms involved in 72% (ZVEH). [pv magazine, Mar 2026](https://www.pv-magazine.de/2026/03/20/elektrohandwerk-verzeichnet-weitere-rueckgaenge-bei-photovoltaik-und-batteriespeichern/)

## 1. Planner time per offer
- Manual PV offer 3 to 6 h versus 20 to 45 min with software; 1.5 to 2.5 h per proposal (vendor claim, Surge, surgepv.com blog; exact URL to add before use).
- Site visit 1 to 2 h. [reduco.ai](https://reduco.ai/blog/solar/pv-anlage-planen-dimensionieren) (blog, 2026)
- Reonic customer: proposal in 10 to 15 min (vendor claim, link above).
- No independent survey of planner design time found. Ask the Reonic experts (question 14 in `expert-questions.md`).

## 2. Installer capacity
- About 60,000 heating installers missing, about 400,000 employed in the SHK trade (ZVSHK, 2023, link above).
- ZVEH (Dec 2025) warns of a future PV skills shortage as firms leave the segment: share of electrical firms active in PV and storage fell from 57.1% to 52.1%; survey of 1,641 firms. [Solarserver](https://www.solarserver.de/2025/12/10/zveh-warnt-vor-fachkraeftemangel-im-photovoltaik-sektor/)
- Waiting times of 3 to 9 months for a heat pump slot appear only on advice sites (low quality, do not use as a headline).
- Nuance: rooftop PV demand fell in 2025, so pitch productivity per planner (more offers, faster, more consistent), not only the shortage.

## 3. Speed and win rate
- No residential-solar study found that links offer speed to signing rate.
- Generic lead response: contacting a lead within 1 hour makes qualifying about 7 times likelier (Oldroyd, HBR 2011). [BYU ScholarsArchive](https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/facpub/9711)
- An often-repeated "5 min versus 30 min, 21 times likelier" figure comes from 2007 vendor data (MIT/InsideSales). Avoid it, or label it.
- The challenge brief itself states that "the first credible offer often wins" (Reonic Hackathon Challenge, p. 5). Citable as Reonic's view.

## 4. Market size
- Heat pumps: 299,000 sold in 2025, +55% (BWP, link above).
- PV: about 17.5 GW added in 2025 (17.7 GW in 2024); private rooftops 5.2 GW; rooftop additions down about 25% (BSW). [pv magazine, Jan 2026](https://www.pv-magazine.de/2026/01/05/bsw-solar-mehr-neue-photovoltaik-anlagen-auf-freiflaechen-und-weniger-auf-daechern-2025/)
- Rooftop PV systems 2025: about 450,000 (ZVEH, link above).
- Installed base: just under 4.8 million PV systems in Germany, early 2026. [Solarserver, Mar 2026](https://www.solarserver.de/2026/03/12/fast-fuenf-millionen-photovoltaikanlagen-in-deutschland-installiert)
- Home batteries: about 580,000 installed in 2024; electrical trade fitted 235,000 in 2025 versus 260,000 in 2024 (ZVEH, link above).
- EU 2024: 65.5 GW PV, residential 12.8 GW. [pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2024/12/18/eu-solar-installations-hit-65-5-gw-in-2024-says-solarpower-europe)

## 5. Cost of design errors
- Verbraucherzentrale NRW: do not oversize the battery; rule of thumb 1 kWh per 1,000 kWh yearly household use. [pv magazine, 2023](https://www.pv-magazine.de/2023/08/22/verbraucherzentrale-nrw-die-fuenf-groessten-irrtuemer-bei-photovoltaik-heimspeichern/)
- No euro figure or return rate for badly sized systems found. Ask the experts (question 16).

## 6. Reonic's own claims
- Enquiry to offer in five minutes; 3,000+ customers (reonic.com).
- EUR 13M Series A, September 2024, led by Northzone. [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/cleantech-startup-reonic-raises-14-million-for-solutions-to-accelerate-clean-energy-deployment/)
- "13 markets" is from the challenge brief; not confirmed on the website.

## Back-of-envelope value for one installer
All inputs are labelled. Replace the assumptions with the experts' answers when we have them.

| Input | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Offers per planner per week | 10 | assumption (brief says "several offers per day") |
| Time saved per offer | 1 h | conservative pick below the cited 1.5 to 2.5 h vendor range |
| Planner cost per hour | EUR 40 | estimate, not sourced |
| Working weeks per year | 45 | assumption |

Per planner: 10 x 1 h x 45 = 450 hours a year, about EUR 18,000, or roughly 11 extra working weeks to spend on site or on more offers. On the high vendor figure (2.5 h saved) this becomes 1,125 hours. Treat as illustrative until the Reonic experts confirm the real time per offer.
